        [ 02 // PROBLEM MATRIX ]
      

      

        FIVE BOTTLENECKS IN GLOBAL FINANCE
      

      

        Enterprise finance handles trillions of dollars across millions of complex contracts. 
        Here is how traditional RPA fails, and how RAG & Agentic AI completely resolve each friction point.
      


      

        
        

          

            [ 01 ]
            Accounts Payable
          

          
Autonomous Invoice Exception Handling & 3-Way Matching

          

            The Bottleneck: Service invoices (milestone billing, consulting hours) use non-standard terminology that mismatches SOW rate cards and triggers costly manual review queues in ERPs.


            The AI Solution: RAG indexes SOW rate schedules and semantic aliases. The Contract Validator Agent confirms rates and milestones, while the ERP Agent verifies PO balances and Goods Receipts in SAP S/4HANA.
          

          
RAG Clause Grounding + Auto-Dispute Drafting

        


        
        

          

            [ 02 ]
            Accounts Receivable
          

          
Intelligent Remittance Processing & Unallocated Cash

          

            The Bottleneck: Clients send bulk lump sums with messy remittance PDF notes and arbitrary withholding deductions, leaving millions unallocated.


            The AI Solution: Remittance Agent parses messy PDFs and executes combinatorial invoice matching against open ledger items.
          

          
Combinatorial Clearing Engine

        


        
        

          

            [ 03 ]
            Treasury Compliance
          

          
Multi-Jurisdiction Sanctions & ISO 20022 Compliance

          

            The Bottleneck: False-positive AML/OFAC flags delay international wire settlements across 170+ countries.


            The AI Solution: Screening Investigator Agent indexes global sanctions registries and cross-references beneficial ownership (UBO) records to clear false positives in seconds.
          

          
Autonomous Sanctions Triage

        


        
        

          

            [ 04 ]
            Cloud & Consulting
          

          
Contract-to-Billing Dispute Resolution

          

            The Bottleneck: Disputed cloud usage overages and SLA downtime credits hold up multimillion-dollar vendor invoices.


            The AI Solution: Claims Analyzer Agent queries live infrastructure telemetry logs and calculates contractual credits pursuant to signed SLA appendices.
          

          
Telemetry-Verified SLA Credits

        


        
        

          

            [ 05 ]
            Liquidity Ops
          

          
Working Capital & Dynamic Payment Scheduling

          

            The Bottleneck: Rigid ERP payment runs fail to capture 2/10 Net 30 early payment discounts or minimize foreign exchange (FX) conversion costs.


            The AI Solution: Liquidity Forecast Agent predicts incoming collections and optimizes payment execution across FedNow, RTP, and SWIFT rails.
          

          
Dynamic Discount Optimization

        

      

    


    
    

      

        [ 03 // COGNITIVE ENGINE ]
      

      

        THE MULTI-AGENT AP SWARM
      

      

        Rather than relying on brittle rule engines, four autonomous subagents collaborate under an AP Supervisor to inspect, cross-verify, and resolve every invoice line item.
      


      

        

          

            
[ AGENT 01 ]

            
Normalizer Agent

            

              Ingests raw PDF/EDI/JSON invoices. Standardizes role descriptions, parses milestone tags, and detects discrepancies between billed rates and line totals.
            

            
TOOLS: Regex Parser, Currency Normalizer

          


          

            
[ AGENT 02 ]

            
Contract Validator

            

              Executes hybrid RAG vector search over MSAs, SOW rate cards, and clauses. Semantically aligns informal roles to contracted ceilings with confidence scoring.
            

            
TOOLS: BM25 Retriever, Semantic Role Aligner

          


          

            
[ AGENT 03 ]

            
ERP Action Agent

            

              Queries SAP S/4HANA Procurement APIs. Validates PO committed balance, checks line allocations, and verifies approved Goods/Services Receipts (SES).
            

            
TOOLS: SAP BAPI Mock, PO Ledger Balance

          


          

            
[ AGENT 04 ]

            
Resolution Agent

            

              Evaluates policy tolerances (≤ $25 tax variance). If variance exists, autonomously drafts vendor dispute notices citing contract clauses, or notifies PMs for missing receipts.
            

            
TOOLS: Policy Gate, Dispute Synthesizer
